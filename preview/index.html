Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our botanical formulas, shipping, and guarantees.

Are Glowérive formulas safe for sensitive skin?
Yes! Every single product is dermatologist-tested, hypoallergenic, and formulated without synthetic fragrances, drying alcohols, parabens, or sulfates. We specifically formulate for reactive and sensitive skin barriers.
How fast is shipping and is it free?
We offer Free Express Shipping on all orders over $50. Orders are dispatched within 24-48 hours and typically arrive within 3 to 5 business days with full tracking information provided.
Are your products 100% cruelty-free and vegan?
Absolutely. We never test on animals at any stage of production, and all raw ingredients are sustainably wildcrafted or organic plant-derived with Leaping Bunny certification standards.
In what order should I apply the products?
Always apply from thinnest to thickest texture: Cleanser ➡️ Toner ➡️ Serum (Radiance Boost) ➡️ Moisturizer (Hydra Glow Gel Cream) ➡️ Daily UV Sunscreen (morning) or Night Cream (evening).
What is your 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee?
If you don’t notice a real improvement in your skin’s texture and radiance within 30 days, simply contact our support team for a hassle-free full refund. No questions asked.
